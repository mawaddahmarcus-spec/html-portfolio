The Best Movies according to Mawaddah

My Top 5 Movies

1. KPOP Demon Hunters

A thrilling adventure into the world of K-Pop, where a group of talented idols must band together to fight off supernatural forces threatening their industry.

2. Nevertheless

A poignant exploration of love and relationships, following the lives of two art students as they navigate the complexities of their feelings for each other.

3. My Oxford Year

A heartwarming tale of love and self-discovery, as a young woman embarks on a life-changing journey while studying at Oxford University.

4. Purple Hearts

A romantic drama that follows the love story between a struggling musician and a soldier, exploring themes of sacrifice and resilience.

5. Outlander

A captivating tale that blends romance, adventure, and historical drama, following the journey of a World War II nurse who is mysteriously transported back in time to 18th-century Scotland.
